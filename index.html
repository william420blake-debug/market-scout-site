Market Scout

Market Scout is a research tool designed to help evaluate product and niche opportunities across online marketplaces using public marketplace data, approved APIs, and clearly labeled estimates.

What Market Scout does

  • Researches product and niche opportunities.
  • Compares observable pricing and competition signals.
  • Ranks opportunities using configurable scoring criteria.
  • Separates verified facts from estimates and inferences.
  • Stores historical research so changes can be tracked over time.

Etsy integration

Market Scout is being developed for personal, internal research use. Etsy data is accessed through Etsy's approved Open API where available. The tool does not claim access to private seller information and does not fabricate unavailable metrics such as exact sales or search volume.

Current scope

The current version focuses on marketplace research and recommendations. It does not automatically purchase products, publish listings, place advertising spend, contact customers, or perform other seller actions without human approval.

Independence

Market Scout is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Etsy, Inc.
